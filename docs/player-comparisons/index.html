Offensive drill comparisons

WR, TE, OT, OG and C only. Each plot uses one recorded attempt per player, chosen closest to that player's median eligible attempt duration. Blue highlights a player with a typical duration, not the best performer.

Alignment: movement begins at the first of three consecutive frames at or above 1 yard/second. Paths are translated to zero and rotated so the first 2-yard displacement points forward. Left/right signs are preserved; drill labels and positions remain separate. This does not distinguish facing direction or guarantee identical drill protocols.

Speed: dark line = median; shaded band = middle 50% of players. These summaries stop when fewer than 80% of players remain recorded. Faint individual curves continue to their recorded ends.

Turn panel: an exploratory candidate, not a verified route break. It selects the largest direction change between half-second displacement vectors (at least 35 degrees), with at least one second recorded on either side. Each half-second vector must span at least 0.6 yards and center speed must be at least 1 yard/second. It can pick a finishing maneuver or a different turn across players, especially in multi-turn drills. Do not interpret it as a validated performance metric.

Axes vary between drills. Full records can include drill exits. No NFL performance relationship has been tested here.
